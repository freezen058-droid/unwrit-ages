Unwrit Ages is a turn-based 4X strategy game in the spirit of Civilization V. Found a city, work the land around it, research your way from the Ancient era to the Modern, and win by conquest, culture, science or diplomacy — whichever suits the empire you actually built.

It is a complete game. No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no energy timers, nothing to buy inside it. You install it and you play it.

WHAT IS IN IT
- The full Civ V-style ruleset: cities, citizens, tiles and yields, a complete technology tree, social policies, religion, espionage, city-states, great people, diplomacy and war.
- An original tileset drawn for this game, from the grassland under your first settler to the seventeen natural wonders.
- Ten languages: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português (Brasil), Русский, Polski.
- A guide for new players that gathers the game's sixty tutorials into six readable chapters, and a small teaching game to try them in.

THE OPTIONAL WALLET FEATURES
Unwrit Ages can connect to a Solana wallet on your phone — Phantom, Solflare, or any other Mobile Wallet Adapter app. This is entirely optional, and the whole game is playable start to finish without ever opening the Wallet screen. Your seed phrase never touches this app.

Connecting one makes two things possible:

- Recording a save on-chain. This writes the save's name and its SHA-256 fingerprint to Solana, so anyone can verify later that exactly that save existed at exactly that moment. The save file itself never leaves your device. It costs 1 SKR, only ever for a save you named yourself, and autosaves are never charged.
- Minting a victory certificate. Win a game and you can mint an NFT recording who won, by which victory, on which turn and in which year, owned by your wallet. It costs US$0.75, converted to SOL at the moment you mint, plus the network's own cost of roughly US$0.17 — the rent for the account that holds it. The certificate carries its record inside itself rather than pointing at a website, so it does not go blank if some server disappears.

Those two are the only charges this game will ever ask you for, and neither happens without your wallet asking you to approve it first.
