One settler at the edge of an unwritten world. Six thousand years to make it yours.

Drop your first city on a river, on a hill, or right on the border of a neighbour who's going to regret it. Everything that follows — every war, every wonder, every betrayal — grows out of that first move.

Spread wide and risk thin walls, or build tall and dare the world to come for you. Beeline Bronze Working, or gamble on Writing. Found a religion before your rival does. Buy a city-state's loyalty, tear up an alliance, and march your longswordsmen over the hill at exactly the wrong moment — for them.

WIN IT. THEN OWN IT.
Saves get lost. Phones get replaced. Your greatest victory shouldn't disappear with them.

Connect a Solana wallet — Phantom, Solflare, or anything that speaks Mobile Wallet Adapter — and mint your win as an on-chain certificate: who won, how, on which turn, in which year. The record lives inside the certificate itself, in your own wallet, so there's no website to go dark and no link to break.

US$0.90, converted to SOL at the moment you mint, plus roughly US$0.17 the network charges to keep it.

Played a game you'll want to prove happened? Stamp a save on-chain — its fingerprint and a timestamp, never the file itself — for 1 SKR. Autosaves are recorded only if you switch that on, and every record asks you first.
