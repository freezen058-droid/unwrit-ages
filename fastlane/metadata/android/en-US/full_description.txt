Build a civilization from a single settler to a world power. Found cities, research
technologies, work the land, choose your policies, and meet every other leader on the map
across the whole sweep of history.

CivilWars is a turn-based 4X strategy game: you take your turn, everyone else takes theirs,
and nothing happens until you are ready. No timers, no ads, no energy meters.

* A full technology tree, policy trees and a complete set of units and buildings
* Randomly generated worlds, or hand-made maps in the built-in editor
* Terrain rendered as lit relief - hills, mountain ranges, floes and marshes are drawn from
  real height fields, so the map reads at a glance
* Plays offline, on the phone or the desktop, and saves are yours to keep
