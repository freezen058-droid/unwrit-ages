It starts with one settler and an empty map.

Where you plant that first city decides the next six thousand years. Work the land around it. Raise wonders your neighbours will envy and then besiege. Push your people from the ancient world to the modern age across a full technology tree, and shape who they become along the way — with social policy, with religion, with spies in other people's capitals.

Then take the world the way that suits the empire you actually built. By conquest. By culture. By science. Or by never firing a shot.

NOBODY IS SELLING YOU ANYTHING
No ads. No accounts. No analytics. No energy timers. No storefront behind a menu, no second currency, nothing that finishes faster if you pay. You install it and you play all of it.

WHAT YOU GET
- A full 4X campaign: cities and citizens, tiles and yields, a complete technology tree, social policies, religion, espionage, city-states, great people, diplomacy and war.
- Terrain drawn for this game and nothing else, rendered as lit relief with several variants of every tile — from the grassland under your first settler to all seventeen natural wonders.
- Ten languages: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português (Brasil), Русский, Polski.
- A guide for new players that gathers sixty tutorials into six readable chapters, and a small starter game to try them in.

AND WHEN YOU WIN, YOU KEEP IT
Save files get lost. Phones get replaced. The victory doesn't have to live in one.

Connect a Solana wallet — Phantom, Solflare, anything speaking Mobile Wallet Adapter — and mint a certificate of that victory: who won, by which victory, on which turn and in which year, held in your own wallet and readable by anyone. It carries its record inside itself rather than pointing at some website, so it doesn't go blank the day a server does.

US$0.75, converted to SOL at the moment you mint, plus roughly US$0.17 the network charges to keep it.

You can also put a save on-chain: its name and its cryptographic fingerprint, timestamped, so you can prove later that exactly that game existed at exactly that moment. The save file itself never leaves your phone. 1 SKR, and only ever for a save you named yourself — autosaves are never charged.

THE HONEST PART
Those two are the only charges this game will ever ask you for, and neither happens without your wallet asking you first. Your seed phrase never touches this app. The whole campaign — every victory, every era, every language — is playable start to finish without ever opening the Wallet screen.
